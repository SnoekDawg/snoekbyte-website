BackorderPRO decides a variant's inventory policy (`CONTINUE` or `DENY`) and the
delivery information shown to customers. Two layers of rules drive this.

## The rule engine

- **Inventory rules (per feed):** conditions on feed columns or Shopify data
  (stock level, price, compare-at price). Actions set `CONTINUE` (allow selling)
  or `DENY`.
- **Global rules (store-wide):** a safety net for variants that no feed updates
  anymore — for example old or removed SKUs. They can set inventory policy,
  stock status, clear delivery dates and set `preorder_allowed`.

Rule limits per plan: Free/Starter 2, Growth 10, Business unlimited.

## Delivery date & lead time

- The **expected delivery date** is read from a mapped feed column.
- **Lead-time ranges** (min/max with a unit of days, weeks or business days) can
  be set globally and **per vendor** *(Growth+)*.
- Values are written to variant metafields (`snoek_bpo.delivery`,
  `vendor_lead_time`, `stock_status`, `preorder_allowed`).

## Pre-order settings

Enable pre-orders with the master toggle, then configure:

- **Button styling:** custom text, subtitle, icon, colours and click action
  (add to cart / notify me / custom URL).
- **Minimum price:** only allow pre-order at or above a price.
- **Maximum lead time (days):** deny pre-order when the supplier's expected
  restock date (the feed source date, excluding the per-feed buffer) is further
  than X days in the future. Leave empty for no maximum.
- **Allow in-stock pre-order** (optional).
- **Per-vendor overrides** *(Growth+)*: enable/disable, minimum price and maximum
  lead time per supplier, with a `_default` fallback.

The original Add-to-cart button can be hidden on pre-order products
(configurable). Products the supplier actually has in stock are never forced to
`DENY` by the pre-order gates — an existing `CONTINUE` from a stock rule is
preserved.

## Tracking backorders *(Growth+)*

Every order is checked on creation; negative-inventory lines are stored as
backorder sales with a snapshot (SKU, barcode, vendor, quantity, price, stock).
The **Tracker page** groups open backorders per order with status buckets
(on-track / due-soon / overdue / snoozed / resolved), search, vendor and sort
filters, bulk snooze/resolve, per-order notes and CSV export.

## Keeping customers informed *(Starter+)*

Customer emails for backorder and delivery updates are available on Starter and
up, with **11 built-in template languages** (EN, NL, FR, DA, ES, CS, PL, DE, PT,
IT, SV). Customer email addresses are encrypted at rest and never shared with the
AI model.
